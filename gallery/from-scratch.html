Make the Harbour Street bus lane permanent

In the 12-week trial, Route 14 crossed Harbour Street up to 4.6 minutes faster, and car delay on every affected street stayed inside the board’s 2-minute limit.

Decision today: $185,000 for permanent works from the 2026–27 bus priority capital line, so the lane opens on 6 April 2027.
