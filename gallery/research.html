Three limits keep this study from setting the threshold on its own

  1. 22 leaks

    Too few leaks for a precise rate

    Sensitivity at 0.50 could be anywhere from 57% to 90%, a 33-point range. Every comparison between thresholds inherits that width.

  2. 1 district

    One district, one month

    Eastfield in March: wet ground and busy night-time pumping. Summer nights and plastic mains carry leak noise differently.

  3. 38 sites

    Quiet sites were not excavated

    A leak the survey team could not hear is counted as no leak, so the true sensitivity may be lower than 77%.
